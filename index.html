Ads Money

An iOS app that shows your Google AdMob, AdSense and YouTube estimated earnings in one private dashboard.

Read-only access · No Ads Money server · Your data stays on your device

What Ads Money is for

Ads Money is a mobile earnings tracker for app publishers and creators. Its single purpose is to let you view, on your iPhone, the advertising revenue that Google already reports to you across AdMob, AdSense and YouTube — without opening three separate dashboards.

One clear overview

Estimated AdMob earnings for today, yesterday, this month and over time, with breakdowns by app, country, ad format, ad unit and platform, plus mediation reports.

AdSense & YouTube in one place

Your unpaid AdSense balance, payment history, and YouTube revenue trends with top videos and country splits.

How Ads Money uses your Google data

  • You sign in with Google and grant read-only reporting scopes (AdMob, AdSense, YouTube Analytics).
  • Your device requests reports directly from Google's APIs and renders them on screen.
  • Ads Money operates no server, database or analytics backend — your earnings data is never uploaded to us, sold, or shared, and report data is kept only on your device.
  • You can revoke access at any time from the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Full details in the Privacy Policy, including our Google API Services Limited Use disclosure.

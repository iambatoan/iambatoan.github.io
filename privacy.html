Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026 · Ads Money

Ads Money ("the app", "we") is an iOS application that displays your Google advertising and YouTube earnings. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what never happens with it.

The short version

Ads Money has no server. Your reports travel directly between your device and Google's APIs over an encrypted connection, with read-only access, and are kept only on your device for the current session. We cannot see, collect, sell or share your earnings data — it never reaches us.

Data accessed from your Google Account

When you sign in with Google, the app requests read-only access to:

  • Basic profile (name, email, profile photo) — shown in the app so you know which account is connected.
  • AdMob reporting data (admob.readonly) — estimated earnings, impressions, eCPM and related reporting metrics.
  • AdSense payment data (adsense.readonly) — unpaid balance and payment history.
  • YouTube Analytics monetary reports (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly) and YouTube channel metadata (youtube.readonly) — revenue trends, top videos and country splits. These scopes are optional and only requested if you choose to connect YouTube.

All access is read-only. The app cannot modify your ads, campaigns, videos, payments or any other part of your Google account.

How that data is used

  • Reports are fetched from Google's APIs directly by your device and rendered on screen.
  • Report data is held in the app's memory for the current session. A small amount of preference data (selected account ID, reporting time zone, appearance and theme choice) is stored locally on your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any Ads Money server, because none exists. There is no analytics backend, no database and no cross-device sync operated by us.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

Ads Money's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is used only to provide the earnings-viewing features you see in the app, is never sold, is never used for advertising targeting, and is never read by humans.

Advertising

The free version of Ads Money shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ad requests only start after Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow allows it in your region, and Google's consent state determines the ad treatment you receive. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes ad data.

Purchases

Ads Money Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details.

Revoking access

  • Sign out in the app (Settings → Sign out) to remove the Google session and local account selection from your device.
  • You can additionally revoke Ads Money's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Ads Money is intended for publishers managing Google advertising accounts and is not directed at children under 13.
